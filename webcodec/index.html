Royalty-free video, end to end

Playing a format the browser does not know takes two steps: decoding it, and re-encoding the result into something the browser does play. The decoders below are royalty-free — Theora and Dirac both are — but the usual encoder is not: H.264 is pooled, and libx264 is published as licence-required.

So the encoding is handed to the browser itself. mm_solver carries GPAC's WebCodecs filters as built-ins, and use-webcodec="enc" asks for the encoder only: the picture is decoded by the WebAssembly accessor named in with, then encoded to H.264 by the codec your browser already ships. Nothing patent-encumbered is downloaded.

The "enc" matters. A bare use-webcodec also registers the WebCodecs decoder, which puts itself forward for the input track, fails on a codec WebCodecs does not know — Theora, for one — and does not hand back to the accessor you asked for.

What works, and what does not yet

Measured against mm_solver 1.0.2, this is where the WebCodecs encoder stands for the four royalty-free video families:

FormatDecoderThrough WebCodecs
Theoratheora_1 works on short clips
Diraclibschro_1 works
MPEG-1 / MPEG-2libmpeg2_1 refused: Failed to connect mpeg2vdec to wcenc: Bad Parameter
Motion JPEGlibjpeg_1 refused: Filter not found for the desired type

The two refusals are in the encoder, not in the decoders, and neither can be worked around from the page. wcenc requires WIDTH and HEIGHT on its input at connection time, which mpeg2vdec only knows after parsing a sequence header; and no pixel-format conversion resolves between libjpeg's output and what the encoder accepts.

The clips here are deliberately short. A long or large source starves the session while the asynchronous WebCodecs callbacks are in flight, GPAC reports No data received from fetch for 5000 ms with an idle session and ends early, leaving a truncated file — the same 1080p Theora runs complete at 4 seconds and stops after 1 second when given 33.

The same thing, without touching the page

The browser extension applies this to any site: it watches for media the browser cannot decode and runs the same pipelines, with the same rule that only royalty-free accessors are used unless you say otherwise.

The sample files and formats have been selected because they are licence-free to use. All demos are downloaded in their entirety, please be patient.
