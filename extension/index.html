What your browser cannot read

Every file below is embedded with plain HTML — an ordinary <img>, <audio> or <video> tag, no Bevara attributes, no scripts, no WebAssembly. Exactly what a site that never heard of Bevara would write:

<img src="testcard.psd">

So this page measures your own browser. Each card says who rendered it: your browser on its own, or the Bevara Universal Media extension stepping in where it could not.

Video, decoded and re-encoded live

Video is the hardest case and the best demonstration. Whatever the source, the result has to be handed back to the browser as something it plays, so each of these is decoded by a WebAssembly accessor and then re-encoded to H.264 by your own browser, through WebCodecs. Nothing patent-encumbered is downloaded, and these play with the extension's default settings — no option to tick.

Two limits, both in the solver and both being worked on: the re-encoded video has no sound, and a long source comes out truncated — which is why these clips are short. Formats whose decoder is itself patent-encumbered (HEVC, VVC, AV1, VP9, AVS, EVC) stay out of reach until you allow encumbered accessors in the options; you will find them below, reported as unavailable.

No browser reads these

42 formats. Nothing to fall back on, no amount of waiting fixes it — the decoder has to come from somewhere. This is the archive case: files whose publisher is long gone.

Some browsers read these, and yours may not

19 formats. This is the cost a site pays today: store several encodings, write chains of <source>, and hope. And support can be withdrawn — Chrome carried Ogg/Theora for fifteen years and dropped it in version 123.

And a format published today is readable today

The extension carries no decoder of its own. It reads a catalogue from the Bevara filter registry and fetches what a page needs, so a format becomes readable the day it is published — not when a browser vendor gets to it. JPEG XL was finalised in 2021 and Chrome still does not read it; AV1 took five years; HEIC never arrived.
